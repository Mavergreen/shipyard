no-cross-products: a native/-cross product pair is one universal product under the native short name.

## Why
Architectures A5: a product runs on every architecture its macOS floor reaches, so a build for another
architecture is a slice of the same product and not a second one. A product whose short name ends in cross, or
whose line does, makes a consumer choose between two products by asset name.

## Incident
2026-10-14: five native/-cross pairs made consumers choose by asset name, fd and rg by uname -m (architectures survey §3)

## Limits
It reads .mavergreen/products/ only.

## Deviating
No deviation is read.
